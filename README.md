# Customer Churn Analysis — Telco Dataset

## Problem Statement
A telecom company is losing customers every month. 
The goal is to identify key factors driving churn 
and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle — IBM Telco Customer Churn
- Size: 7,032 customers, 21 features

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Key Findings
1. Month-to-month contract customers churn 3x more 
   than two-year contract customers
2. New customers (tenure < 6 months) have the 
   highest churn rate
3. Churned customers had higher monthly charges 
   (median ~$80) vs retained customers (median ~$65)
4. Fiber optic customers churn the most — possibly 
   due to high cost
5. Senior citizens have a higher churn rate compared 
   to non-senior customers

## Business Impact
- Monthly revenue loss identified: $139,130
- Yearly projected loss: $1,669,570

## Business Recommendations
1. Offer discounts to convert month-to-month 
   customers to annual plans
2. Improve onboarding experience for new customers
3. Create retention offers for high-paying customers
4. Investigate fiber optic service quality/pricing
5. Design senior citizen specific retention programs
